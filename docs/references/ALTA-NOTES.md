## Useful product patterns

- Capture/classify owned items before expecting useful outfit suggestions.
- Keep saved looks and collections durable, rather than leaving them as chat responses.
- Connect actual wear history to understandable observations.
- Treat travel as dated outfits plus an item-derived packing list.
- Translate inspiration into owned-wardrobe combinations with visible gaps.
- Keep private use possible even with social discovery.
