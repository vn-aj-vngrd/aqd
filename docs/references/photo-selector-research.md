# New piece: empty photo selector research

Research retrieved October 6, 2026. Research followed by an exploratory Paper comparison and user-approved adoption in canonical V1 L04. No app code changed. Context: [capture photo](../design/capture-photo.md), [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md), and AQD design quality/rules. The starting composition is the user-described small white portrait block with image icon, “Choose photo”, “No photo selected”, and separate “Take photo”; it was not independently inspected in Paper.

## Apple evidence and the iOS 27 boundary

- **iOS 27 is publicly documented in the sources retrieved**, not merely assumed: [Apple’s iOS page](https://www.apple.com/os/ios/) is titled “OS – iOS 27”; [Apple security releases](https://support.apple.com/en-us/100100) lists iOS 27 on September 14, 2026 and 27.0.1 on September 28. This verifies public version information, **not a new iOS-27-specific photo-selector design**. No dedicated iOS 27 empty upload treatment was established in this research.
- **Do not invent a “Photos pickers HIG” citation.** The attempted `/design/human-interface-guidelines/photos-pickers` URL returned 404. Use the actual [PhotosPicker API](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/photosui/photospicker), Apple’s [WWDC20 Photos picker session](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2020/10652/) and [WWDC22 update](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2022/10023/) for selection behavior. The latter explicitly recommends the system picker over a custom picker and demonstrates an app-owned trigger opening system selection.
- **Privacy:** WWDC20 says the picker runs outside the app process, needs no direct library access or library permission prompt to display, and returns only user-selected media. This is distinct from requesting PhotoKit library authorization. Keep native library selection; do not add a broad-library permission preflight. Camera permission is a separate concern, not covered by this privacy guarantee.
- **Selection is not completed import:** WWDC22 explains that bound selections are placeholders; asset loading can take time or fail, including unavailable iCloud downloads. Filter to images and one selection for one AQD piece. Keep local preparation/progress/cancellation distinct from the no-photo state; preserve fields and prior accepted media on failure. These are API/contract requirements, not an aesthetic reason to build a custom library grid.
- **Content is not glass:** Apple’s [HIG Materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials) says “Don’t use Liquid Glass in the content layer” and reserves it for the distinct controls/navigation layer. Its retrieved [documentation JSON](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials.json) identifies the Liquid Glass guidance update as September 9, 2025. Use flat opaque photo/form surfaces and OS-owned picker/navigation chrome, not a frosted upload card.
- **A tappable placeholder should be a control:** [HIG Image views](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/image-views) recommends a system-provided button displaying the image when interaction is needed, rather than adding button behavior to an image view. This supports a clearly labelled, accessible photo-selection button; it does not prescribe AQD’s dimensions.

## Research recommendation: a quiet, actionable photo well (superseded)

Refine the existing portrait well rather than adding a new upload panel or source-selection step:

1. Retain the centred **168 × 224 pt, 3:4** viewport and 4 pt photographic corners. Change the white empty ground to AQD’s opaque **image-ground** token; no dashed outline, shadow, gradient, glass or sample garment.
2. Make the entire empty well one native **PhotosPicker-labelled control**: a restrained SF photo symbol plus blue **“Choose photo”**. Remove visible “No photo selected”; the empty well already conveys absence. Give VoiceOver the equivalent state, “Choose photo, no photo selected”, without separately focusing the decorative symbol.
3. Keep **“Take photo”** plainly visible below as the secondary native-camera action, with the established action spacing and at least 44 pt hit bounds. Do not hide both sources behind an “Add” menu or introduce a custom sheet merely for visual polish.
4. On accepted selection, replace empty content with the **complete image in Fit**, preserving proportions and neutral padding. Keep Change and optional Edit photo separate; never auto-crop to fill the portrait well. Photo, Name and Category remain mandatory; Save stays disabled until accepted local media and valid fields exist. Picker/camera cancellation, denied camera access and failed imports preserve the draft.

**Why this direction:** clearer affordance with less redundant copy; continuity between empty and selected framing; AQD restraint rather than borrowed competitor branding. Dimensions, tint and copy are a design recommendation derived from AQD’s contract—not Apple mandates. A larger viewport is unnecessary until native testing demonstrates a need.

## Exploratory compact alternative in Paper (historical comparison)

At the user's request, the main session created an unchanged comparison copy and a compact alternative on [Paper page 01 · V1](https://app.paper.design/file/01M3SM2KBHBZG6MA0Q744Y9NW0/p-2-0), to the right of Home and first piece. Artboard names: **Compare · Photo selector · Original** and **Proposal · Photo selector · Compact**. This tests a different composition from the conservative research recommendation above; neither is an Apple-mandated pattern.

The proposal uses a full-width 350 × 88-point selection row with a photo symbol, Choose photo and disclosure chevron; the entire row is intended as one native PhotosPicker control. Take photo remains a separate 44-point camera target below. No portrait preview or redundant no-photo text appears before acceptance. After successful import, return to the existing selected-photo 3:4 Fit preview with Change/Edit; do not auto-crop. Loading, cancellation, denial, failure, replacement, required fields and draft protection retain the existing capture contract. VoiceOver should expose one Choose photo button with no-photo state; decorative symbols are not separate focus stops. Save remains disabled until accepted media/name/category exist. Name/Category move 136 points higher, while the save group remains bottom-aligned at the reference size; native layout must still scroll and adapt to keyboard/accessibility text.

The user subsequently approved replacement of canonical L04. The compact section now lives in the retained L04 artboard, and both exploratory comparison artboards were deleted. Capture/component/flow/interaction contracts and the manifest record this scoped adoption; shared masters and other capture drawings were not changed. The main session inspected the final canonical screenshot and computed styles. The user then requested a single Add photo entry instead of a separate camera action. Canonical L04 now has only Add photo, opening new L159's native source sheet with Choose from Photos and Take photo. Close/outside tap/swipe preserves the draft; wider layouts adapt to a popover. This direct replacement supersedes the two-source form composition above; no additional research was performed. It is an approved static design, not working native functionality.

**Evidence limits / acceptance:** no competitor app was installed or exercised, no current empty-selector screenshot was verified, and the research agent performed no AQD canvas/runtime audit; the later limited static comparison is recorded above. Apple JavaScript documentation required public JSON retrieval for HIG text. Before adoption, test discoverability of the whole-well tap, Dynamic Type/VoiceOver, dark/Increase Contrast, keyboard-safe scrolling, native picker/camera cancellation and denial, iCloud progress/failure, and landscape/square/extreme-aspect Fit. Do not mark native behavior or conversion improvement as verified from these sources.
