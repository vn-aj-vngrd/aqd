# Pricing and profitability hypotheses

Updated October 1, 2026. We start from zero users. The user has not selected paid tiers, feature limits or a subscription price. This document records proposals to validate, not entitlements to implement.

## Proposed model

Freemium: let people experience useful private wardrobe work and community, then charge for recurring styling/planning convenience. Trial price hypothesis: **$4.99/month or $49.99/year**, subject to launch-market willingness-to-pay research and actual capability quality.

| Free proposal | Plus proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Capture/organize pieces, manual outfits/plans, actual wear/basic history. | More extensive assisted recurring-office and travel/packing workflows. |
| Discovery, sharing, follows, human chat and basic assistance. | Advanced wardrobe insights and personalized styling workflows. |
| Useful core experience before upgrading. | Higher media allowances only if validated costs justify them. |

Do not charge for unverified Agent capability. No upload quota or premium entitlement is approved here; reconcile future decisions with existing product/feature defaults. Keep owned records readable/exportable after cancellation. A subscription's recurring value must be demonstrated, not inferred from its AI label.

## Illustrative economics

Assume all subscribers pay $4.99 monthly and an eligible enrolled developer pays a 15% Apple commission. Approximate proceeds per subscriber before tax/refunds/other expenses: **$4.2415/month**. Annual plans change the result: $49.99 × 85% / 12 ≈ **$3.54/month equivalent**; cash arrives on a different schedule.

| Hypothetical paying subscribers | Monthly proceeds after assumed commission |
| --- | ---: |
| 500 | ~$2,121 |
| 1,000 | ~$4,242 |
| 2,500 | ~$10,604 |

These are arithmetic scenarios, not forecasts. At 50k MAUs they represent 1%, 2% and 5% paying shares, not download-to-paid cohort conversion rates. We do not expect 50k users at launch.

A hypothetical $500 infrastructure bill needs about 118 monthly subscribers to cover it alone. A hypothetical $3,500 total monthly expense budget needs about 826, before taxes/refunds. Neither is our approved launch spending plan. Use the actual [launch budget](COSTS.md) for current planning; hosting coverage is not full business profitability.

Apple's 15% assumption requires qualification and enrollment in the [Small Business Program](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/). Storefront terms, taxes and adjustments can affect actual proceeds.

## Validate from launch

Measure successful first saves, repeated outfit/planning/wear use, premium task demand, paid conversion, renewals, cancellation/refunds and support burden. Track actual contribution after store fees and variable service costs; include compensation, content, moderation and acquisition when judging profit.

Show an upgrade at a demonstrated need after the user has received value. Learn from a small cohort of retained paying users before increasing advertising spend. Measure acquisition cost and payback rather than assuming social sharing produces free growth. Keep cloud AI costs separately metered if that capability is ever selected.
